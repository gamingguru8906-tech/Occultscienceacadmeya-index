Questions about giving

Will I get a receipt? add

Yes. Razorpay emails an instant payment confirmation, and we send a trust receipt separately within three working days. If neither arrives, write to us and we will trace the transaction.

Is my donation tax deductible? add

Only if we hold a valid 80G certificate — check the registration panel above for the current position. We would rather tell you plainly than let you assume a benefit that does not exist. Please confirm your own eligibility with a tax adviser.

Why is there a minimum of five for the food drives? add

Below five portions, the fuel and transport cost more than the food. Batching keeps the ratio honest. If five is more than you can give, the open donation form has no minimum at all.

Can I see where my money actually went? add

Yes. Every quarter's ledger lists income, expenditure and the count of people served, with receipts. Ask for the breakdown on any specific drive and we will send it.

Does donating improve my horoscope or remove doshas? add

No. Feeding the hungry is a good thing to do, but we do not teach that it magically cancels your karma or changes planetary movements. Give because it helps someone today, not because you are trying to bribe the universe for tomorrow.
