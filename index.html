Our Promise

The Transparency Guarantee

Spirituality attracts more than its share of people willing to take advantage. We built this trust to be the opposite of that. Here is exactly how your money is handled — check it, question it, and hold us to it.

01

A registered, accountable trust

Occult Science Academya is a charitable trust registered under the Indian Trusts Act. Our registration certificate, trust deed and trustee list are published on this page. Verify us before you give us a single rupee — we would rather you did.

02

100% transparent, quarter by quarter

Every quarter we publish a public ledger: what came in, what it bought, how many people it reached, and the receipts behind it. Photographs from each drive are dated. Nothing is summarised into a vague "impact figure".

03

Your donation goes to the cause, not the overhead

Our trustees personally absorb administration, website and staffing costs out of their own consultation income. When you fund a khichdi meal, that money buys khichdi. The only deduction is the payment gateway fee, which Razorpay charges and we show you openly.

04

Zero tolerance for cheating

Fear-based selling is grounds for immediate removal from this academy — no warning, no second chance. That applies to every reader, teacher and volunteer who carries our name, including the founders.
